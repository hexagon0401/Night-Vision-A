# Synthetic detection fixture

case.json is generated by tools/generate_detection_fixture.py. All sensors, measurements, timestamps, claims and point locations are fictional. Three 49-hour series include a large final spike, a sustained small shift and a stable control; three nearby fixture points exercise co-occurrence. Source hashes refer to JSON-serialized synthetic value arrays produced by the generator, not real publisher content.

These are deterministic integration fixtures, not historical calibration samples, verified intelligence or a measurement of real-world precision. The API/CLI carry synthetic origin into the analysis report. Physical points are labelled synthetic even though coordinates are valid WGS84 numbers. No real asset locations are asserted.
